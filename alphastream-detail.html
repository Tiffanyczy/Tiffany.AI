AlphaStream

An earnings-call intelligence platform that tracks live and upcoming calls, surfaces verified transcripts with sentiment, and keeps a personal watchlist of tracked tickers.

About this Project

AlphaStream keeps earnings season in one place. The Home view shows Live & Upcoming calls for companies with real-time status, the quarter being reported, and the consensus EPS estimate for each.

Alongside that sits a feed of Trending Transcripts: verified, sentiment-tagged write-ups of each call (Bullish, Bearish, Neutral) so you can gauge the tone of a call without listening to the full recording. A Watchlist view tracks a custom set of tickers with live price, trend, and next-call status, and a global ticker search jumps straight to any company's earnings details.
